Hello! Nice to meet you and welcome. I am a Postdoctoral Research and Teaching Assistant at the [ADAGIO](https://www.loria.fr/fr/la-recherche/les-equipes/adagio/) group of LORIA and the University of Lorraine. My research interests lie in Discrete Geometry and Image Processing.

A few projects I have worked on:
- Quantum Brush (2025): A project exploring the intersection of physics and art by using quantum control theory and variational algorithms to generate unique digital brushes. [GitHub](https://github.com/mothorchids/Luminists-Quantum-Brushes)  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24173)
- PhD Thesis (2021–2023): Funded by the [PARADIS](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/tristan.roussillon/paradis.html) research project at [LIRIS](https://www.insa-lyon.fr/) in Lyon, under the supervision of [David Coeurjolly](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/david.coeurjolly/) and [Tristan Roussillon](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/tristan.roussillon/). My work focused on developing variants and conducting theoretical studies of plane probing algorithms.
- Medical Image Recognition (2020): Worked on image recognition challenges in medical applications.
- Pattern Comparison (2019): Solved partial differential equations for a pattern comparison problem.
- Soft Robotics (2018): Developed mathematical models for soft robotic hands.
